Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026. Scannistic is made by Filip Waikiki, Prague, Czechia.

Scannistic does not collect any data. There is no account, no server, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you scan ever reaches the developer.

The camera

Scannistic uses the camera only to photograph the document in front of it. Finding the page, straightening it and reading its text all happen on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device frameworks.

Your scans

  • While you are scanning, the pages are kept inside the app on your iPhone, encrypted by iOS whenever the phone is locked and excluded from backups. They are deleted as soon as the scan is saved.
  • A saved scan is a PDF in the folder you chose. If that folder is in iCloud Drive or another cloud service, the file is stored by that service under its own terms — the same as any other file you put there.
  • Email and Share hand the PDF to the app you pick (Mail, Messages, AirDrop…). Scannistic sends nothing by itself.

Settings

Your preferences — the folder, the file name, the categories, an address to put in the To field — are kept on your iPhone and nowhere else.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. Everything Scannistic keeps goes with it; the PDFs you saved stay in your folder until you delete them there.
