Scannistic

Scan paper straight into a folder, as a searchable PDF.

Support

Questions, a scan that came out wrong, an idea: kokpit250@gmail.com.

Where do my scans go?

Into the folder you choose when Scannistic first opens — in iCloud Drive, on the iPhone, or any service that shows up in the Files app. Change it any time in Settings. Categories can each have a folder of their own.

Can I search inside a scan?

Yes. The text on every page is read on your iPhone and hidden under the picture, so the PDF can be searched and its text selected and copied.

The edges of the page were found wrong

Drag the four corners on the Adjust crop screen — the magnifier shows exactly where each one sits. Pages can be re-cropped later from All Pages.
